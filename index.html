MODULE_04B // FREQUENTLY ASKED
When do I pay?

Payment timing depends on project size. Smaller projects are typically invoiced upon completion. Larger builds may use a milestone structure agreed upon before work starts.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept standard payment methods including bank transfer, check, and major payment apps. Details are provided on your invoice. We do not require you to keep a card on file.

What if the project scope changes?

We talk first. If you request additional features beyond the original scope, we provide an updated estimate before any additional work begins. You approve it, then we proceed.

Do you charge for consultations?

Initial consultations are typically free for small businesses evaluating our services. If a consultation requires extensive research beyond a standard discovery call, we'll let you know upfront.

What about hosting and domain costs?

Hosting and domain registration are separate from the design and build fees. These can be bundled with your ongoing site fees or set up directly through third-party providers. We will help you get everything configured and ensure all recurring costs are completely transparent.

Is there a maintenance fee after launch?

We do not charge automatic maintenance fees. If you need updates after launch, we quote those separately on a per-request basis. You only pay for what you actually need.
