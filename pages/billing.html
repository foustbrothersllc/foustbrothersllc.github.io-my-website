BILLING PROTOCOL

TRANSPARENT. MANUAL. NO SURPRISES.

Foust Brothers operates on a simple billing philosophy: you know exactly what you're paying before any charge is made. No subscriptions. No auto-billing. No hidden fees. Every invoice is manual, reviewed, and approved by you first. Your project is our work order — and work orders get itemized.

⚡
MANUAL INVOICING ONLY

Every charge is issued manually. No automatic payments are ever initiated. You receive an invoice, review it, and pay on your terms. We do not store payment information.

◈
TRANSPARENT PRICING

Before any work begins, you receive a clear scope and price. What we quote is what you pay. If scope changes, we talk first — no surprise line items appear on final invoices.

●
NO AUTO-CHARGES

We will never automatically charge your card or bank account. Period. Every payment requires your explicit action. You are always in control of your billing.

▲
PERFORMANCE-BASED

We tie our billing to completed work and client satisfaction. Milestone-based payment structures are available for larger projects so you pay as value is delivered.

◉
ITEMIZED RECORDS

Every invoice includes a clear breakdown of work performed, hours if applicable, and deliverables. You always have documentation of exactly what you paid for.

⊕
LOCAL ACCOUNTABILITY

We're a local operation. If you ever have a billing question, you reach a real person — not a support ticket queue. Disputes are resolved directly, fast, and fairly.

MODULE_04B // FREQUENTLY ASKED
When do I pay?

Payment timing depends on project size. Smaller projects (1–3 pages) are typically invoiced upon completion. Larger builds may use a milestone structure: a portion upfront to cover initial work, with the remainder due on delivery. This is always agreed upon before work starts.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept standard payment methods including bank transfer, check, and major payment apps. Details are provided on your invoice. We do not require you to keep a card on file.

What if the project scope changes?

We talk first. If you request additional features or pages beyond the original scope, we provide an updated estimate before any additional work begins. You approve it, then we proceed. No scope creep billing.

Do you charge for consultations?

Initial consultations are typically free for small businesses evaluating our services. If a consultation requires extensive research or detailed technical scoping beyond a standard discovery call, we'll let you know upfront if there's an associated fee.

What about hosting and domain costs?

Hosting and domain registration are separate from our build fees and are billed directly through third-party providers (e.g., Vercel, Namecheap, GoDaddy). We help you set these up and will clearly explain any recurring costs so you're never surprised.

Is there a maintenance fee after launch?

We do not charge automatic maintenance fees. If you need updates, edits, or support after launch, we quote those separately on a per-request basis. You only pay for what you actually need.
