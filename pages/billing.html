BILLING PROTOCOL

TRANSPARENT PRICING · MANUAL INVOICING · NO SURPRISES

We believe in complete transparency when it comes to billing. No hidden fees, no automatic charges, no surprise costs. You approve every invoice before payment is processed.

Our billing protocol is designed to give you control and clarity throughout your project. You'll always know exactly what you're paying for and why.

PAYMENT TERMS
DEPOSIT

50% deposit required to begin work. This secures your project in our schedule and covers initial development costs.

PROGRESS PAYMENTS

For larger projects, milestone-based payments may be arranged. Each milestone is clearly defined in your scope of work.

FINAL PAYMENT

Remaining balance due upon project completion and before final delivery. Final approval required before payment.

OUR COMMITMENT
MANUAL INVOICING ONLY

Every invoice is manually created and sent for your review. No automated billing systems, no recurring charges without explicit agreement.

ITEMIZED PRICING

Every invoice includes a detailed breakdown of charges. You'll see exactly what each component costs and why.

NO AUTO-CHARGES

We never charge your card automatically. You must approve each invoice before payment is processed.

ACCEPTED PAYMENT METHODS
CREDIT / DEBIT CARD

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover

BANK TRANSFER

Direct bank transfer / ACH available

CHECK

Business or personal checks accepted

CASH

In-person payment available by appointment

// IMPORTANT NOTE

All billing terms are clearly outlined in your project scope of work before any work begins. We encourage you to review the scope carefully and ask questions. Clear agreements prevent misunderstandings and ensure a smooth project experience.
